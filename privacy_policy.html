Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

Banquerito ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance and tax-planning application for expats, freelancers, and autónomos. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes, where it is stored, and what third-party services it relies on.

In short: Banquerito does not operate its own servers and does not store your accounts, transactions, or financial data anywhere except your own device. There is no cloud sync and no user account system. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly send to the optional AI Consultant feature, and anonymous requests to a public currency exchange rate service.

1. Data We Collect and Where It Lives

  • Financial data you enter — accounts, balances, transactions, planned payments and income, and your tax profile. This data is stored exclusively in a local database on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.
  • Attached files — documents, receipts, or images you attach in the app (e.g. to the AI Consultant) are saved to the app's private storage on your device.
  • AI Consultant conversations — chat messages you send to the AI Consultant, and the account/tax context needed to answer them, are stored locally on your device so your conversation history survives app restarts.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — currently, Banquerito does not use any crash-reporting or analytics service. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated in advance.

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identifiers to use the app.

2. Third-Party Services

Banquerito integrates with the following external services. Each is used only for the specific purpose below, and each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Anthropic Claude API — powers the in-app AI Consultant. When you send a message to the Consultant, the text of your message, any attached file content, and the financial context needed to answer your question (account balances, tax profile, currency rates) are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use the Consultant feature — nothing is sent automatically or in the background. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Currency exchange rate API — used to fetch current exchange rates for currency conversion within the app. These requests do not include any personal or financial data — only a request for current rates.

3. Planned Future Integrations

We are evaluating an optional Open Banking integration (e.g. via Plaid) to let you automatically import transactions from your bank instead of entering them manually. If and when this feature ships:

  • It will be entirely opt-in — the app will continue to work fully without it.
  • You will connect your bank directly through the provider's secure authentication flow; Banquerito will never see or store your banking password.
  • This Privacy Policy will be updated before the feature is released, describing exactly what data is accessed and how it is used.

4. Data Storage and Security

All financial data, planned payments, tax settings, and chat history are stored in an on-device SQLite database and in the app's private file storage. This data is sandboxed by the operating system and is not accessible to other apps. We have no server infrastructure that stores or processes your financial data — there is nothing for us to be breached, because we simply don't hold it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You can delete individual accounts, transactions, or the entire chat history at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data, including the local database and any attached files — there is no server-side copy to clean up.

6. Children

Banquerito is a financial planning tool intended for adults managing their own income, accounts, and tax obligations. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly design any part of the app to collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

Because all financial data is stored only on your device and under your control, you can exercise most privacy rights directly within the app:

  • View, edit, or delete any account, transaction, or planned item at any time
  • Clear your AI Consultant chat history at any time
  • Remove all data permanently by uninstalling the app

If you have questions about how the Anthropic API or the exchange rate service handle data sent during your active use of the app, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above, or contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves — for example, when the Open Banking integration described in Section 3 becomes available. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
